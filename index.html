The go / no-go structure

Verify the work.
Then release payment.

Payment decisions follow the agreed quality gate. When a milestone is not verified, the associated payment does not move forward.

Authorize payment.

NO-GO / Not verified

Hold. Resolve. Recheck.

Address the issue and return for verification before the associated payment is released.

Illustrative explanation of the go/no-go model. Project-specific scopes, verification requirements, authorities, and payment terms must be documented for each engagement. This is not a guarantee of cost, schedule, or investment performance.

Do we need to work through Embrace Your Shine?+

No. The Embrace Life Cottage Campus is one partnership configuration. The MicroLiving model can also be used by other entities, with responsibilities and services established for each project.

What does MicroLiving coordinate?+

The approach connects development planning, general contracting coordination, construction management, execution partners, and quality checkpoints. The project scope determines the exact responsibilities.

How are milestone payments handled?+

Under the go/no-go structure, payment release follows verification by the Quality Control Officer. Work that does not pass the checkpoint returns for resolution and another review before the associated payment is authorized.

Can the model start with a single site?+

Yes. The scalable approach spans single units, multi-unit clusters, and community-scale programs. Suitability, scope, and project terms still need to be established for the particular site.
